Unleash your dog's inner explorer!

TrackPaw turns tracking training into something you can see and measure. Lay a track with your phone, let it rest, then watch how your dog follows it — whether you're a seasoned trainer or just getting started with nosework.

KEY FEATURES

- Lay tracks: Walk your route with your phone and TrackPaw records the path on the map. Put the phone in your pocket — recording continues in the background.
- Follow tracks: When your dog runs the track, TrackPaw records the route you actually took, so you can compare it to the laid track side by side.
- Training statistics: See how long the track took to lay, how long it rested, how long your dog took to finish it, and how long the trail was.
- Notes and difficulty: Rate each track's difficulty and write feedback, from both the track layer and the tracker.
- Share tracks: Invite a training partner to follow a track you laid. They see it on their own phone, and you both see the result.
- iCloud sync: Your tracks stay in sync across your devices, stored in your own iCloud account.

TrackPaw has no ads, no account, and no tracking beyond the tracks you choose to record.
