TrackPaw needs no account or login. Tracks are stored on the device and synced through the user's own iCloud account (CloudKit).

Background location ("Always") is used only while the user is actively laying or following a track, so the phone can be put in a pocket during a training session. Tracking stops as soon as the user taps Stop. No location data is sent to the developer or any third party.

To test: tap "Add track", then "Lay track" and walk a short distance, then Stop. Open the track and tap "Follow track" to record the dog following it. Sharing a track (Share button on a track) requires being signed in to iCloud.
